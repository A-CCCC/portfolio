Photos for the Modular Car Container page.

Drop photos in here and the page shows them, in filename order, in place of
its "coming soon" note. The start of a filename puts a photo in a section:

    problem-01.jpg     -> The Problem
    solution-01.jpg    -> The Solution
    first-01.jpg       -> The Process: First Attempts
    measure-01.jpg     -> The Process: New Measurements
    grid-01.jpg        -> The Process: Modularity

anything else goes in the gallery at the foot of the page. For work in progress, make a subfolder per stage —
see public/photos/PAGE-PHOTOS.md. Everything in public/photos/<page>/ is
picked up automatically; nothing needs editing in the code.

Photos straight off a phone are large: say so and they can be made
web-sized, with the originals kept out of public/ (in source-photos/).
